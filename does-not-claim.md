# The "does not claim" fence

Maps of Bharat publishes official numbers, mapped and cited. It does not interpret them. This fence defines what the product will and will not assert — it governs page copy, social captions, and moderation.

## The site presents, it does not conclude
- **No causation.** A map shows *what* is distributed, never *why*. Never state or imply that one variable causes another ("high X because of Y").
- **No verdicts on places or people.** A ranking is an ordering by a measured value, not a judgment. Never frame "worst" / "best" as a verdict about a district's people, governance, or worth.
- **Estimates are not exact.** Inherited / projected / re-aggregated values are disclosed as such at the point of use, and never presented as precise measurements.
- **Numbers as reported.** Values are shown as the official source reported them, harmonised to current boundaries and cited. Disagreements with a source are logged as corrections, not silently changed.

## What is allowed
- Neutral, sourced framing of *which* story to tell (adr-028): choosing a metric and a region for a card is editorial; the number and its classification stay neutral and disclosed.
- Stating measured facts with their source, year, and methodology.
- Disclosing limitations and withheld data.

## Why it exists
It keeps the product credible and legally safe, and it unblocks captions and moderation: a caption that stays inside the fence needs no case-by-case judgment, and a comment demanding a causal verdict can be answered plainly — the site presents the number and its source; it does not claim the cause.

The public statement of this stance lives on `/terms` and `/methodology`; this file is the internal reference for anyone writing copy or moderating.
