# Response playbook

How to reply to comments and messages on Maps of Bharat's social posts. Pairs with `moderation-policy.md` (what to allow/remove) and `does-not-claim.md` (what the product asserts). Voice per `VOICE.md`: impersonal, factual, brief.

## The one rule
**Correct once, with a source. Then stop.** The product's credibility comes from the numbers and their citations, not from winning threads. A single sourced correction serves every future reader of the thread; a back-and-forth serves no one.

*(The owner reserves case-by-case judgment on whether to engage at all. The default, absent a reason to do otherwise, is the rule above.)*

## By comment type
| Comment | Response |
|---|---|
| "This number is wrong" (with a real basis) | Verify. If they're right, thank them, correct it, and log it on `/corrections`. If our number is right, reply once with the source + year, then stop. |
| "You should show X / add Y" | If reasonable, acknowledge and note it for the roadmap. No promise of timing. |
| "Why is this district high/low?" (asking for causation) | Do not supply a cause. One line: the map shows what is distributed, not why; here is the source. Then stop. |
| "This proves [political claim]" | Do not endorse or refute the politics. Restate the fence once if useful; do not argue. |
| Abuse, slurs, threats, harassment | No reply. Remove per `moderation-policy.md`. |
| Bad-faith bait / concern-trolling | No reply. Hide if it derails; otherwise ignore. |
| Praise / good-faith question | Reply briefly and warmly, still impersonal and sourced. |

## Never
- Never claim a cause or a verdict to win a point (breaks the fence — the whole credibility model).
- Never argue past the first sourced correction.
- Never disclose personal identity in-thread (the brand is faceless; outreach emails may be signed, replies are not).
- Never delete a *valid factual* correction — turn it into a corrections-log entry instead.

## Escalation
- Coordinated brigading or a viral misread → stop replying, let the pinned source stand, and consider a single neutral clarifying post. Do not chase individual comments.
- Legal/defamation threat → do not respond in-thread; capture it and handle off-platform.
