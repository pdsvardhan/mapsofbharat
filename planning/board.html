Decided against & parked

so they don't get relitigated
No user-generated content, everAvoids IT-Rules intermediary duties (adr-029). Only inbound is the private corrections report.
No accounts until a paid tier needs themThe map is free to read without a login, forever (adr-028).
No display ads, no early sponsorshipMonetise via data-access, licensing, commissioned viz — never ads.
No paywall on viewingPaid buys the table/data + export, never reading the numbers on the map.
No GADM / global boundary filesSurvey-of-India compliant boundaries only — non-compliant files are a legal risk.
Bivariate map — parkedPost-launch; curate pairings at build time.
Election-constituency map — heldUntil after the 2027 delimitation (current PC/AC boundaries expire).
Satellite metrics — parkedOnly if land-cover / rainfall / elevation is pursued.
SHRUG night-lights + EC13 — droppedCC-BY-NC licence incompatible with ads / a paid tier (adr-024, #204).
No Zenodo DOI · never self-cite on WikipediaCredibility comes from official sources, not self-citation.
No paid legal advocate (for now)Own AI + research at trademark time.
No teaching as a revenue leverOut of the monetisation set.
Hostile threads: correct once, then stopA sourced correction serves every future reader; arguing serves no one.
Religion on the site, never on socialToo easily weaponised out of context in a feed.
Deferred / low-priority: #384 (LGD swap when monetising) · #386 (elections district-level) · #390 (doc-freshness) · #157 (RBI QSDCB, login-walled) · #380 (flaky test → folds into #405) · #412 (corrections dedup).
