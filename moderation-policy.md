# Moderation policy

Maps of Bharat hosts **no comments or user content on the site** (adr-029). This policy therefore governs **comments on the project's own social posts** (Instagram, X, LinkedIn, etc.), where discussion happens.

## Principles
- **The fence sets the terms.** Every post stays inside the "does not claim" fence (`does-not-claim.md`): a number, its source and year, neutral framing — no causal claims, no verdicts on places or people. A post that stays inside the fence rarely needs defending; a comment that demands a verdict can be declined on principle, not opinion.
- **Comments stay on** (owner decision). They are not published back onto the site, so there is no intermediary exposure — the site remains UGC-free.
- **Correct, don't argue.** Factual corrections (a wrong number, a mis-cited source) are welcome and get a sourced reply or a corrections-log entry. Interpretation fights are not the project's job.

## What to expect, by topic
- **Society & crime metrics** carry the **highest comment load** — crime rates, religion-adjacent demography, and inter-state comparisons attract heat. Post these knowing moderation time will be higher; keep captions strictly inside the fence.
- **Religion** appears **on the site** (it is official Census data) but is **never posted to social**. It is too easily weaponised out of context in a feed.
- **Rankings** are the most misread format — a "bottom 25" is an ordering by a measured value, never a judgment. If a ranking post can't be framed without implying a verdict, don't post it.

## Handling comments
- **Factual error flagged** → verify; if correct, reply with the source and, if it's our data, log it on `/corrections`.
- **Bad-faith / abusive** → hide or delete per the platform's tools; do not engage. Slurs, harassment, and threats are removed without discussion.
- **Demands for a causal/political verdict** → do not supply one. If worth answering at all, restate the fence once ("the site presents the number and its source; it does not claim the cause") and stop.
- **Off-topic / spam** → remove.

See `response-playbook.md` for how (and whether) to reply.
