# Which HOTSPOT metrics actually want a symbol map — per-metric audit

_Generated by research task R3 on 2026-08-20 for to-do #531. Feeds `planning/BUILD-PLAN-408-symbol-maps.md`
(step 3, "per-metric routing for the 29 HOTSPOT metrics") and Stream S4. Read-only audit: no code, no
database and no metric was modified. Background: `research/758-choropleth-classification-findings.md`
(map-form brief, Q6 area-bias) and `research/2026-08-04-c7-choropleth-classification.md` (classification)._

---

## The question

`BUILD-PLAN-408` says 29 district metrics are HOTSPOT-type and "cannot be shown honestly today", and
proposes proportional symbols for all of them. The plan's own closing section flags the doubt:

> Which of the 29 metrics genuinely want symbols versus which want a rate denominator instead is a data
> question. […] several may be better fixed by normalising to per-capita than by changing the chart type.

So, per metric: is it a **count** that only symbols can show honestly; a **count with a denominator we
already hold**, where the honest fix is a new rate metric and the choropleth stays; or an **already-normalised
rate** that got flagged for skew or zero-inflation, where symbols are simply the wrong tool and the C7
classification work is the right one?

## Headline

**Far fewer than 29 want symbols. Four do.**

| Verdict | Count | Share of the 29 |
|---|---|---|
| **SYMBOL** — genuine count, no honest denominator left to add | **4** | 14% |
| **DENOMINATOR** — fix by adding a rate metric from a denominator we already hold | **3** | 10% |
| **NEITHER** — already a rate/share/field variable; skew or zero-inflation, belongs to C7 classification | **22** | 76% |

Three-quarters of the "29 metrics that cannot be shown honestly today" are already normalised. They are
percentages, per-100k rates, per-capita values, millimetres of rain and degrees of heat. Nothing about a
proportional-symbol layer helps them, because they never had the area-bias problem symbols solve — a
percentage in Kutch is already comparable to a percentage in Mumbai City. What they have is right-skew and
zero-inflation, which is exactly what `selectMethod` (item 757) and the `log` break method already ship for,
and what the C7 brief's Q2 decision rule is for.

The whole district library contains **only 9 raw counts out of 87 metrics** (units: `hectares`, `tonnes`,
`head`, `birds`, `people`, `km²`). Every other district metric is a rate, share, index or physical field
measurement. Area bias — the thing symbols fix — can only bite those 9. The symbol layer is therefore a
narrow, high-value fix, not a third of the library.

**This does not shrink the case for building #408.** Population, rice, wheat and forest change are four of
the most-wanted maps on the site, and every one of them is currently a lie. It re-scopes the *routing*
work in step 3 of the build plan from 29 metrics to 5.

## Method

### 1. Catalogue and distributions

```sql
-- district metric catalogue (87 rows)
SELECT m.id, m.name, m.unit, m.category, m.default_scale, m.year, COUNT(mv.value) AS n
FROM metrics m
JOIN metric_values mv
  ON mv.metric_id = m.id
 AND mv.region_level = 'district'
 AND mv.value IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY m.id
ORDER BY m.category, m.id;

-- every district observation, for distribution statistics (60,735 rows)
SELECT metric_id, region_code, value
FROM metric_values
WHERE region_level = 'district' AND value IS NOT NULL;
```

Per metric, over non-null district values, computed offline in Python (not in the app, not written back):

- **skew** = bias-corrected Fisher–Pearson G1, `g1 * sqrt(n(n-1))/(n-2)` — matches `scipy.stats.skew(bias=False)`,
  the statistic the C7 brief's Q2 rule specifies.
- **p_zero** = fraction of districts with an exact `0`.
- **max/p90** = top district's value divided by the 90th percentile — a direct measure of "the map is one
  bright district".
- **top-10 share** = share of the national total held by the 10 highest districts.
- **eq_dom** = share of districts landing in the single largest 5-class equal-interval bin (the C7 measure).

### 2. Reconstructing the 29 — and a provenance gap worth recording

**The per-metric HOTSPOT list was never persisted anywhere in the repo.** Verified by exhaustive grep for
`HOTSPOT` across `research/`, `planning/`, `decisions/`, `scripts/`, `lib/`, `app/`, `data-rw/`, `tests/`,
`public_docs/` and `design/`. Only the aggregate survives: `BUILD-PLAN-408` ("29 district metrics — a third of
the district library"), `FEATURES.md:80` ("roughly 29 district metrics — about a third"), and two rows in
`planning/2026-08-05/MoB-master-backlog.csv` — `CNT-03` ("29 HOTSPOT metrics (33% of the district library)")
and `RSK-12` ("**Five of six religion metrics are HOTSPOT**"). `research/c7-analysis.mjs` measures colour
dominance but never emits a HOTSPOT flag.

So the list was reconstructed and cross-checked against those two independent recorded facts. Three
unrelated thresholds each return **exactly 29 of 87** and each excludes exactly one religion metric
(`hindu_pct`), reproducing `RSK-12`:

| Rule | n | Religion metrics | Raw counts included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** `skew ≥ 2.0 OR p_zero ≥ 0.20` (adopted) | 29 | 5 of 6 (hindu out) | 7 of 9 |
| **B** `max/p90 ≥ 2.5` | 29 | 5 of 6 (hindu out) | 8 of 9 |
| **C** `skew ≥ 1.75` | 29 | 5 of 6 (hindu out) | 8 of 9 |

All three agree on **28 metrics**. The single disputed slot is `heatwave_days_40c` (in A only) versus
`livestock_cattle` (in B and C only). **Both are given verdicts below**, so S4 is unambiguous whichever
reconstruction the original used. Rule A is adopted as primary because it is the C7 Q2 rule's own two
statistics (skew and near-zero fraction), and it is the only one of the three whose bucket counts appear in
the headline.

`agri_cropped_area` — the ninth raw count, outside all three reconstructions — is also given a verdict, for
the reason in the routing recommendation: a threshold list splits the livestock and agriculture families
across the boundary, and S4 must not route siblings differently.

### 3. Which denominators do we actually hold — a correction

The task brief assumed "population, households, area_km2 all exist". Checked:

```sql
SELECT id, name, unit, category FROM metrics
WHERE lower(name) LIKE '%household%' OR lower(unit) LIKE '%km%' OR lower(name) LIKE '%area%';

SELECT region_level, COUNT(*) FROM metric_values WHERE metric_id = 'area_km2' GROUP BY region_level;
```

| Denominator | Held? | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Population** | **Yes, district-level** | `pop_total`, Census 2011 PCA, 733 districts, GODL-India. `cattle_per_1000`'s methodology already calls it *"the store's standard denominator, disclosed"* — the pattern is established and documented. |
| **Households** | **No — not at any level** | Zero household-count metrics exist. Everything household-flavoured (`assets_*`, `nfhs5_clean_fuel`, `mgnrega_pct_hh_100_days`) is already a **percentage of** households, not a count. |
| **Area (km²)** | **State only** | `area_km2` has 36 rows, all `region_level='state'`. There is no district area in `metrics`, none in `region_keys`, and none in `public/geo/districts.geojson` (properties are `dt_code, district, st_code, year, st_nm, rid` only). |

**Deriving district area from `pop_total / pop_density` does not work and must not be used.** It is accurate in
aggregate (national 3,069,207 km² derived vs 3,089,174 km² official, −0.6%) but fails badly exactly where
area bias matters most — the huge, sparse districts:

| District | Derived from pop/density | Published Census 2011 area | Error |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leh | 394 km² | ~45,110 km² | −99% |
| Kargil | 188 km² | ~14,036 km² | −99% |
| Kupwara | 663 km² | ~2,379 km² | −72% |
| Kutch | 22,259 km² | ~45,674 km² | −51% |
| Jaisalmer | 39,407 km² | ~38,401 km² | +3% ✓ |
| Barmer | 28,302 km² | ~28,387 km² | ✓ |

State-level roll-up confirms it: Gujarat −11.9% (the Rann), Himachal +3.5%, every other state within ±0.5%.
The J&K/Ladakh figures are not a rounding artefact — `pop_density` itself is wrong there (Leh reads
339 people/km²; the true figure is ~3). **Flagged separately below; out of scope for this task.**

**Consequence:** area-denominated fixes (cropping share, forest-change rate, livestock density per km²) are
currently **blocked on data we do not hold**. Population-denominated fixes are unblocked today.

## Per-metric verdict table

Bucket definitions, applied strictly and identically to every row:

- **SYMBOL** — a genuine count/total with **no honest denominator left to add**: either none exists, or the
  rate companion is *already a shipped metric*. No data work is owed; the fix is the chart. → route to the
  proportional-symbol layer.
- **DENOMINATOR** — a count whose honest rate companion is **missing but computable from a denominator already
  in the DB**. The fix is a new derived rate metric shown as a choropleth, not a chart change. The raw count
  may *additionally* get a symbol layer once the rate ships, but symbols are not the fix.
- **NEITHER** — already a rate, share, or intensive field variable. Area bias does not apply. Symbols are
  wrong. → stays a choropleth; the defect is skew/zero-inflation and belongs to the C7 classification work.

Current form for every row below is the same: **5-class choropleth, break method chosen at runtime by
`selectMethod`**; the stored `default_scale` is shown as a one-frame placeholder only.

## The finding that changes how S4 should think about this

**A per-capita denominator is not a de-skewing device, and treating it as one will produce worse maps.**

Measured on this catalogue, before → after dividing by `pop_total`:

| Metric | skew before | skew after | Verdict on the normalisation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `livestock_buffalo` | 2.18 | **1.18** | Helps |
| `agri_rice_production` | 4.21 | 3.46 | Barely helps, and semantically wrong |
| `livestock_goat` | 3.60 | 3.52 | No effect on skew |
| `livestock_poultry` | 8.71 | 7.74 | No effect on skew |
| `livestock_cattle` | 1.79 | **3.75** | **Worse** |
| `agri_cropped_area` | 1.46 | **1.98** | **Worse** |
| `agri_wheat_production` | 2.66 | **3.91** | **Worse** |
| `pop_total` → `pop_density` (shipped, area-denominated) | 3.15 | **10.63** | **Much worse** |

Only 1 of 7 candidate normalisations reduces skew. Three make it materially worse.

This is not an argument against normalising — it is a correction to *why* we normalise. Eurostat's rule, as
the 758 brief records it, is about removing the **area-bias lie** (a count in a 45,674 km² district is not
comparable to the same count in a 157 km² district), not about spreading colour. Those are the brief's two
independent defects, and the fixes are independent too:

> normalisation fixes honesty · classification fixes legibility · symbols fix the counts that have neither

So a DENOMINATOR-verdict metric is **not done** when the rate metric lands: `goat_per_1000` and
`poultry_per_1000` will both arrive needing `log` classing, and `cattle_per_1000` — shipped months ago —
is still sitting in the HOTSPOT list today for exactly that reason.

## Recommendation to Stream S4 — how to route

1. **Route by unit semantics, not by the HOTSPOT flag.** The flag is a distribution threshold; it splits the
   livestock family (goat/poultry/buffalo in, cattle out) and the agriculture family (rice/wheat in, cropped
   area out) straight down the middle. Shipping goats as circles and cattle as colour would be visibly
   incoherent. The rule that actually works is one line: **`unit ∈ {people, head, birds, tonnes, hectares,
   km²}` → count → symbol-eligible. Everything else → choropleth.** That is 9 metrics, checkable in SQL, and
   it will not drift when the underlying data is refreshed and a skew value crosses a threshold.

2. **Build the symbol layer for 5 metrics, not 29:** `pop_total`, `livestock_cattle`, `agri_rice_production`,
   `agri_wheat_production`, `forest_change_km2`. Add `agri_cropped_area` as a sixth interim until the area
   denominator lands. `forest_change_km2` is signed and needs a diverging two-colour circle spec that
   BUILD-PLAN-408 does not currently describe — decide it before building, not during.

3. **Do the three livestock rate metrics as a data change, not a chart change.** `buffalo_per_1000`,
   `goat_per_1000`, `poultry_per_1000`, denominator `pop_total`, cloned from `cattle_per_1000` including its
   vintage disclosure. No new ingest, no new source, no symbol work. Then the raw counts become the symbol
   layer and the rates stay choropleths — the standard count/rate pairing.

4. **Return 22 metrics to Stream C7 and close them out of #408.** They are already normalised; the symbol
   layer must not offer itself for them. Concretely: the map should refuse to render a symbol mode for a
   metric whose unit is a `%`, `per 100k`, `per 1000`, `F / 1000 M`, `mm`, `days`, `°C`, `µg/m³`, `index`, or
   any `₹/person`-style unit. Assert that in the test suite alongside the existing "layer appears for a
   symbol metric and not for a rate metric" case in the build plan — with a real rate metric that is *also*
   HOTSPOT-shaped (`crime_cyber_rate` or `pop_density`), because that is the case a naive "is it HOTSPOT?"
   check gets wrong.

5. **Do not expect symbols to reduce the classification backlog.** Every DENOMINATOR-verdict rate arrives
   still needing `log`/Jenks. `cattle_per_1000` is the proof: denominator fixed, still HOTSPOT.

6. **Update `BUILD-PLAN-408` step 3** ("per-metric routing for the 29 HOTSPOT metrics") and the "29 metrics
   unlocked" claim in the plan's rationale and in `FEATURES.md:80`. The honest claim is *"fixes the 5–6
   district metrics that are raw counts and are currently drawn as choropleths, and adds 3 missing rate
   metrics"*. The `~7–10 count metrics` figure already recorded in `MoB-master-backlog.csv` was the accurate
   one all along; the 29 figure counts the classification backlog as if it were symbol work.

## Open questions and thin evidence

- **The original 29 is a reconstruction, not a recovery.** Three independent thresholds each return exactly
  29 and each reproduce `RSK-12`'s "five of six religion metrics", which is strong corroboration, but the
  original list does not exist in the repo and cannot be recovered. The 28-metric intersection is certain;
  the 29th slot is either `heatwave_days_40c` or `livestock_cattle` and both are given verdicts, so nothing

- **`agri_rice_production` / `agri_wheat_production` are ruled SYMBOL deliberately, and it is a judgement
